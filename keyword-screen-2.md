### Counting rule

- A result counts as a **listicle** only if its **title** contains **BOTH a numeral AND the word "Ideas"** — the doc's "N ideas" pattern (e.g. "30 Kitchen Ideas").
- A second count, **"numbered lists"**, is recorded where the title is a numbered list of another kind ("27 Free Boho Patterns", "12 Christmas Table Settings", "18 Minimalist Living Room Designs") — same competitive format, different noun. It is shown as a caution, not the verdict.
- **Denominator = results the search tool actually showed (4–8 per query), NOT 12.** The doc's bands assume a full top 12, so verdicts at the top of a band are marked with their density (count ÷ results shown) so the gap is visible.
- Verdict bands per the doc: **0–3 listicles = STRONG, 4–6 = WINNABLE, 7+ = REJECT**.
- Two judgment calls, applied consistently (same as batch 1): (a) a keyword with **5 observed listicles out of only 6–7 shown** (71–83% density) is marked **REJECT (provisional)** — with that density, the remaining results would need to be almost listicle-free to stay under 7 in a full top 12, and the doc says to finalize only the lowest-count keywords; (b) **click-fit** is applied as a separate pass per the doc — a winnable single-item craft keyword is still rejected.

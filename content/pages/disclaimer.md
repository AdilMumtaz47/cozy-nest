## Home inspiration, not professional advice

The ideas on Cozy Nest are **home inspiration**, shared to spark your creativity. They are not professional advice — not from an interior designer, an architect, a builder or any other specialist.

## Measure before you buy or cut

Room sizes, furniture dimensions and material needs vary from home to home. Always **check measurements before buying or cutting anything**, and double-check sizes against your own space rather than relying on a photo or a list alone.

## Specialist work needs a qualified professional

For **electrical, plumbing or structural work**, always use a qualified professional. Do not attempt work that requires a licence, a permit or specialist training based on anything you read here.

## Availability and prices change

Products, materials and prices mentioned in lists may change or become unavailable over time. Cozy Nest cannot guarantee that a specific item will still be in stock, or at the same price, when you go looking for it.

## Every home is different

What looks wonderful in one home may not suit another. Lighting, room size, existing furniture and personal taste all change how an idea turns out. Use the lists as a starting point and adapt each idea to your own space, budget and style.

## About the photos

Photos on Cozy Nest are curated from public web sources to illustrate each idea. If you own a photo used on this site and would like it credited or removed, please get in touch through the [Cozy Nest Pinterest profile](https://www.pinterest.com/cozynestbysayed/) and it will be sorted out promptly.

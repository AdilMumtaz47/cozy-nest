## How Cozy Nest lists are made

Every list on Cozy Nest follows the same careful process, so readers always know what to expect.

## Choosing a topic

Topics are chosen from what people are searching for on Pinterest. The goal is to cover the rooms, styles and seasons readers genuinely care about — from Christmas mantels and cozy bedrooms to small-space bathrooms and patio corners.

## What every list includes

- **30 ideas** on a single topic, each one distinct and practical
- **30 real photos** — one per idea, showing real homes and real styling
- **8 frequently asked questions** answering the practical questions readers actually ask
- **Tip boxes** with hands-on advice for making each look work at home

## Real photos only

Every photo on Cozy Nest is a real photograph. Images are never computer-generated. Before a photo is used, it is checked for quality — it should be clear, well-lit and true to the idea it illustrates.

## Accuracy and corrections

Lists are reviewed before publishing, but mistakes can still slip through. When a reader flags an error, it is checked and corrected as quickly as possible. Readers can report mistakes through the [Cozy Nest Pinterest profile](https://www.pinterest.com/cozynestbysayed/).

## How photos are chosen

Photos are chosen to show each idea clearly and honestly. A photo should match the idea it sits with, show the styling in a real setting, and be pleasant to look at. Photos that are blurry, misleading or unrelated to the idea are not used. If a photo ever turns out not to match its idea, it is replaced.

## Keeping lists fresh

Home decor moves with the seasons, so lists are revisited from time to time. An idea that no longer works well, or a photo that has gone stale, can be swapped for something better. The number of ideas in a list stays the same — thirty — so readers always know what to expect.

## No paid placements

Nobody can pay to be featured on Cozy Nest. No idea, photo or product appears in a list because someone paid for the spot. Lists are chosen on merit alone — what looks good, works well and genuinely helps readers make their homes cozier.

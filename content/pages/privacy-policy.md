## What this site collects

Cozy Nest collects **no personal information directly from visitors**. There are:

- **No accounts** — you do not need to sign up or log in to read anything
- **No newsletter signup** — the site does not collect email addresses
- **No contact form** — there is no form on the site that asks for your details

You can browse every list on Cozy Nest without giving the site any information about yourself.

## How the site is hosted

Cozy Nest is a static website hosted on Vercel, with its code stored on GitHub. Like most websites, the hosting service may keep standard server logs that record visits — for example, the pages requested and the time of the visit. These logs are a normal part of running a website and are not used to identify individual visitors.

## Links to other sites

Cozy Nest links out to Pinterest, and those links take you to Pinterest's own website. Once you leave Cozy Nest, what Pinterest collects and how it uses it is covered by [Pinterest's own privacy policy](https://policy.pinterest.com/en/privacy-policy). The same applies to any other site linked from these pages.

## Information you share by choice

The only way to contact Cozy Nest is by sending a message through Pinterest. Anything you choose to write in such a message is shared with Cozy Nest through Pinterest, and handled by Pinterest under its own policies. Cozy Nest does not ask for, and does not need, any other personal information from you.

## What the site does not do

Cozy Nest does not run accounts, advertising trackers or analytics tools that follow individual visitors around the web. The site does not sell, rent or share visitor information with anyone, because it does not collect any in the first place.

## Cookies

Cozy Nest itself does not set cookies. Links and buttons that lead to Pinterest may involve cookies set by Pinterest under its own policies. See the [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/) for more detail.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time as the site grows — for example, if a newsletter or contact form is ever added. Any change will be reflected on this page.

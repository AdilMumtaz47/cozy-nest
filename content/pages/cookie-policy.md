## Cozy Nest sets no cookies

**Cozy Nest itself does not set cookies** on your device. Browsing the lists on this site does not place any cookies from Cozy Nest in your browser.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website can ask a browser to store on a device, often to remember a login or a preference. Because Cozy Nest does not use accounts, logins or saved preferences, it has no need for cookies of its own.

## Links to Pinterest

Cozy Nest links to Pinterest, and buttons on the site lead to the [Cozy Nest Pinterest profile](https://www.pinterest.com/cozynestbysayed/). When you follow one of those links, Pinterest may set cookies under [Pinterest's own cookie policy](https://policy.pinterest.com/en/cookies).

## Your browser controls cookies

Whether cookies are accepted, blocked or deleted is controlled by **your browser settings**. You can change those settings at any time in your browser's privacy or security options.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the site ever starts using cookies of its own. Any change will be reflected on this page.

## Get in touch

You can reach Cozy Nest two ways: use the form below, or send a message via the [Cozy Nest Pinterest profile](https://www.pinterest.com/cozynestbysayed/).

<!--CONTACT_FORM-->

## What you can write about

- **Idea requests** — a room, style or season you'd like to see covered in a future list
- **Corrections** — a mistake you spotted in one of the lists
- **Photo credit questions** — anything about a photo used on the site

## A quick note

There is **no public email address** for Cozy Nest, so the form above and Pinterest are the only contact routes. Every message is read. Replies may take a little while, but nothing is ignored.

## What happens next

When you send a message, it is read and noted. Idea requests are added to the list of topics to consider for future lists. Corrections are checked against the list in question and fixed where needed. Photo credit questions are looked into and answered as soon as possible.

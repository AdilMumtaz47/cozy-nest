30 Christmas Bedroom Ideas for a Cozy, Festive Retreat

By Cozy Nest · Nov 24, 2026 · 16 min read

Save this list on Pinterest

The bedroom is the one room where Christmas should feel soft, quiet and a little bit magical. These christmas bedroom ideas turn an ordinary room into a festive retreat — plaid bedding, garland-draped headboards, fairy lights and stockings hung by a crackling fire — without ever feeling overdone. The best part is how easy most of them are: swap the bedding, hang a wreath, light a candle, and the room is transformed.

Work with the palette you already love, whether that is classic red and green, snowy white or icy blue, and let one or two hero pieces carry each idea. For the rest of the house, our 30 Cozy Christmas Ideas for a Warm, Glowing Home has matching inspiration for every room.

1. Red Plaid Bedding with Wreath and Garland

Red plaid bedding is the quickest route to a Christmas bedroom. Layer a plaid duvet with a darker plaid throw, add a red cushion or two, and hang a berry wreath above the headboard with garland draped along it. The room instantly reads festive without a single extra decoration.

Keep the nightstand simple — a candle, a small gift box, a few pine cones — and let the bedding do the talking. It is the cosiest way to wake up on a December morning.

02 of 30

2. Green and Red Pillows Under a Garland Headboard

You do not need to change the whole bed to make it Christmassy. Start with plain white bedding, then pile on deep green and burgundy velvet pillows and drape a lit garland across the wood headboard. Sheer curtains threaded with fairy lights soften the whole scene.

This is the easiest version of the look, and the most flexible: remove the pillows in January and the room is back to normal in minutes.

03 of 30

3. Red Bedding with Fairy Lights and a Wreath

A deep red quilt, a striped throw and a wreath framed by fairy lights make a bedroom glow. The lights do double duty — they outline the wreath on the wall and trace the headboard — so the room feels lit from within. A small tree in the corner with gifts underneath completes the picture.

Keep the walls pale so the red can sing. This is the classic Christmas bedroom, and it never goes out of style.

04 of 30

4. A White Winter Bedroom with a Flocked Tree

If red feels too loud, go white. A flocked tree by the window, pale blue cushions, faux fur throws and a garland of frosted greenery turn the bedroom into a snow globe. Paper snowflakes on the wall and a string of warm lights add the magic.

The trick is texture: knit, fur, linen and flocked branches layered together so the all-white palette never feels flat. It is calm, dreamy and wonderfully Christmassy.

05 of 30

5. Stockings Hung on a Bedroom Fireplace

A bedroom fireplace dressed for Christmas is pure luxury. Hang three stockings from the mantel, pile garland along the top, and flank it with small trees. A lit fire, a snowman figurine and a glowing bauble on the hearth make the whole corner feel like a storybook.

If you have no fireplace, the same idea works on a shelf or a headboard rail. The stockings themselves are the decoration — choose velvet ones in red and green for the richest look.

06 of 30

6. Red Plaid Bedding with a Garland Wall

When the bedding is bold red plaid, the wall behind it should join in. Drape garland along the headboard, hang a wreath and a few framed prints, and let red curtains frame the window. A faux fur throw across the foot of the bed softens all that pattern.

Balance is everything here: white pillows and a plain white throw keep the plaid from overwhelming the room. The result is festive, warm and a little bit grand.

07 of 30

7. Burgundy Pillows Under Garland and Fairy Lights

Burgundy velvet pillows are a quieter way into Christmas colour. Against cream linen bedding and a chunky knit throw, they look rich without shouting, especially under a garland strung with fairy lights and a simple green wreath above.

This is the grown-up Christmas bedroom — elegant, calm and easy to live with all season. Add a candle on the nightstand and the room is done.

08 of 30

8. A Plaid Throw on a Tufted Headboard Bed

A single plaid throw across the foot of a white bed is all the Christmas a room needs. Add green and red velvet pillows, a wreath above the headboard and garland along the top, and the whole room feels festive without changing a thing about the furniture.

The tufted headboard keeps it elegant, and the plaid keeps it cosy. It is the perfect balance for a bedroom that has to feel special in December and normal again in January.

09 of 30

9. A Snowflake Bedroom with a White Tree

Big glitter snowflakes on the wall, a white tree dressed in silver baubles and a garland of frosted greenery make a bedroom feel like a winter wonderland. Keep the bedding white and soft, with a pale throw and a few silver cushions, so the snowflakes are the stars.

String fairy lights along the garland for evening sparkle. This look is especially lovely in a small bedroom, where the white palette makes the room feel bigger and brighter.

10 of 30

10. A Bedroom Fireplace with Plaid Bedding and a Small Tree

A dark wood fireplace with stockings, garland and a small potted tree beside it turns a bedroom into a cabin retreat. Plaid bedding in brown and cream picks up the wood tones, and the firelight does the rest.

Style the mantel with candles, a mirror and a little deer figurine, and keep the bedding simple so the fireplace stays the focus. It is the warmest corner in the house on a cold night.

11 of 30

11. White Faux Fur Bedding with a Flocked Tree

White faux fur throws, pale blue cushions and a flocked tree by the window make a bedroom feel like fresh snow. A frosted garland above the headboard, strung with tiny lights, ties the whole look together without a single drop of red.

This is the softest Christmas palette there is. Layer the textures — fur, knit, linen — and the room stays warm even though every colour is cool. Perfect for anyone who loves winter more than tinsel.

12 of 30

12. A Cabin Bedroom with Fireplace and Plaid

A stone fireplace, a lit tree, stockings on the mantel and plaid bedding make a cabin bedroom the cosiest room in the house. Wood beams overhead and a red patterned rug underfoot complete the lodge feeling, while candles on the hearth add a flickering glow.

Keep the bedding simple — plaid duvet, red throw — and let the fire and the tree carry the Christmas. This is the room for slow December evenings and long lie-ins.

13 of 30

13. Plaid Bedding in a Cozy Attic Bedroom

An attic bedroom with sloped ceilings is made for Christmas. Red plaid bedding, a string of fairy lights along the headboard and a small tree in the corner make the low ceiling feel snug rather than small. A faux fur rug on the floor adds the final touch of warmth.

The sloped walls are a gift here — they hold the lights close and make the room feel like a nest. Add a plaid floor cushion and a tray of candles on the bed for the full effect.

14 of 30

14. Plaid Bedding Under an Ornament Garland

Hang a garland heavy with red and gold ornaments above the headboard and the whole bed becomes the decoration. Plaid bedding in red, green and cream echoes the colours below, and candles on both nightstands make the ornaments glint after dark.

This is a maximalist moment, so keep the walls plain and the bedding pattern simple. The garland is the star; everything else just supports it.

15 of 30

15. Red Curtains and Garland Around the Bed

Red curtains, garland wrapped around the bedposts and wreaths on the wall turn a bedroom into a Christmas card. Plaid pillows, a red knit throw and a stack of red gift boxes on the nightstand keep the colour story going.

The trick is to carry the red right through the room — curtains, bedding, gifts — so it feels deliberate rather than accidental. White bedding underneath keeps it fresh instead of heavy.

16 of 30

16. Red Stockings on a Fireplace with Gold Garland

Three red stockings with gold snowflakes, hung from a mantel dressed with gold baubles and greenery, are Christmas in its most classic form. The fire below and fairy lights around the frame make the whole corner glow.

Style the hearth with a sheepskin rug and a stack of books on the nightstand, and the room feels like a hotel suite at Christmas. Simple, elegant and impossible to get wrong.

17 of 30

17. Green Bedding Under a Garland Headboard

Deep green bedding is an underused Christmas idea, and it is gorgeous. Layer it over plaid sheets, add burgundy velvet pillows, and drape a lit garland across a barn-wood headboard. A wooden bowl of gold baubles on the bed is the finishing touch.

Green feels fresh and forest-like where red can feel loud. It pairs beautifully with wood, brass and candlelight, and it looks just as good in January as it does in December.

18 of 30

18. Red Velvet Bedding with Wreaths Above the Bed

Three matching wreaths with red velvet bows, hung above a white bed dressed with a red velvet throw, make a statement that is both bold and tidy. The symmetry is the secret — identical wreaths, evenly spaced, with a garland draped from the ceiling above.

Keep the bedding white so the red can breathe, and add a white faux fur cushion for softness. This is the look for anyone who loves Christmas to look as good as it feels.

19 of 30

19. A White Bedroom with a Flocked Tree by the Window

A tall flocked tree in the corner of a white bedroom, dressed in white and gold baubles, is the definition of a winter wonderland. White bedding, a faux fur throw and a frosted garland above the headboard keep the palette pure.

The snowy window behind the tree does half the decorating. This room is calm, bright and quietly festive — perfect for light sleepers and lovers of minimal Christmas.

20 of 30

20. Plaid Bedding with a Lit Tree by the Window

A lit tree in the corner, plaid curtains and a plaid duvet in red and green make a bedroom feel like a Christmas morning all month long. Wreaths on the window, garland on the sill and candles on the radiator shelf add layer after layer of warmth.

The plaid-on-plaid works because the scales differ — big checks on the bed, smaller checks on the curtains. Add a cream knit throw to soften the edges.

21 of 30

21. Plaid Pillows and a Garland Nightstand Tray

Plaid pillows in brown and cream, a lit garland along the headboard and a tray of pine cones and candles on the bed make a bedroom feel gently festive. The palette is warm and neutral, so the Christmas touches feel like a whisper rather than a shout.

This is the look for a bedroom that needs to stay calm. The garland and the candles are the only decorations, and the room is better for their restraint.

22 of 30

22. Holiday Pillows with a Plaid Throw

A mix of holiday pillows — holly embroidery, gold branches, red velvet — piled against a wood headboard turns a plain white bed into a Christmas scene. A plaid throw across the foot and a wreath above tie it together, with a wicker basket of books and a candle at the end of the bed.

The pillows are the easiest swap in the whole house. Change them in December, change them back in January, and the room transforms twice with almost no effort.

23 of 30

23. Chunky Knit Bedding in a White Christmas Bedroom

A chunky knit throw the width of the bed, cream knit cushions and a flocked tree in the corner make a white Christmas bedroom feel like a warm hug. The textures do all the work — no colour needed beyond the soft silver of the tree baubles.

This is the cosiest idea on the list. The knit is so thick it looks warm just to look at, and the white palette keeps the room feeling light and airy.

24 of 30

24. Velvet Stockings on a Marble Fireplace

Two velvet stockings with faux fur cuffs, hung from brass hooks on a marble fireplace, are Christmas luxury in miniature. Pine cones, eucalyptus and fairy lights along the mantel keep it natural, while the bed beside it waits in soft white and burgundy.

Choose stockings in deep jewel tones — burgundy and forest green — and the marble stays elegant instead of cold. It is a small detail that makes the whole room feel expensive.

25 of 30

25. Plaid Bedding in a Rustic Wood Bedroom

In a wood-panelled bedroom, red plaid bedding feels right at home. Hang a string of baubles along the headboard, light the garland above it, and put a lit tree in the corner. The wood walls do the rest, glowing amber in the fairy lights.

A small embroidered cushion adds a personal touch. This is rustic Christmas at its best — warm, simple and completely unforced.

26 of 30

26. Three Wreaths Above Red Pillows

Three identical wreaths with red velvet bows, hung in a row above the bed, are the simplest statement in this whole list. Below them, red velvet pillows and a cream textured cushion on white bedding keep the look clean and calm.

Symmetry is what makes it work. Measure the spacing, hang the wreaths at the same height, and the wall looks like it was designed, not decorated.

27 of 30

27. An Icy Blue Bedroom with a White Tree

Icy blue bedding, a white faux fur throw and a white tree dressed in silver baubles make a bedroom feel like a frozen lake at sunrise. A lit snowflake on the wall and tiny bottle-brush trees on the nightstand complete the winter scene.

Blue is the coolest Christmas palette, and it works best with lots of white and silver. Keep the lights warm, not cool, so the room feels magical rather than chilly.

28 of 30

28. Stockings and Garland on a Stone Fireplace

A row of patterned stockings under a garland heavy with red baubles is the heart of a Christmas bedroom. The stone fireplace behind them and a lit tree beside them make the corner feel like a country inn.

Mix the stocking patterns — floral, plaid, solid red — for a collected-over-years feel. The garland above should be generous, spilling over the mantel edge with berries and lights.

29 of 30

29. Plaid Bedding with a Tree on the Nightstand

A small lit tree on the nightstand, plaid pillows and a garland along the headboard make a bedroom festive without a full-size tree. Pine cones on the nightstand and a lamp glowing beside them keep the corner warm and intimate.

This is the best idea for a small bedroom or a guest room. One small tree, one garland and a set of plaid pillows are all it takes.

30 of 30

30. Grey Plaid Bedding Under a Garland Headboard

Grey plaid is the modern way into Christmas bedding. Pair it with white sheets, a chunky grey knit throw and a garland of frosted greenery and white baubles draped over a white headboard. Candles on the nightstand add the warmth.

The grey keeps the room calm and contemporary, while the garland and baubles say Christmas clearly. It is the perfect look for anyone who wants festive without the traditional red.

Key Takeaways

  • A Christmas bedroom does not need a full redecoration — it needs a few bold, warm choices.
  • Swap in plaid bedding, drape a garland over the headboard, hang a wreath or two and light some candles, and the room becomes a festive retreat.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I decorate my bedroom for Christmas on a budget?

Start with the bed: a plaid throw and two festive pillows cost very little and change the whole room. Add a paper snowflake garland, a string of fairy lights and a candle or two. Thrifted wreaths and homemade stockings keep costs down, and most of these pieces pack away neatly for next year.

What colours work best in a Christmas bedroom?

Classic red and green with white is the most traditional and the easiest to find. White and silver feel calm and wintry, while icy blue with white is fresh and modern. Burgundy and forest green look rich and grown-up. Pick one palette and repeat it in the bedding, the garland and the tree for a pulled-together look.

How can I make a small bedroom feel Christmassy?

Use the bed as the canvas: plaid bedding, festive pillows and a garland along the headboard. Add one small tree on the nightstand or in a corner, and a string of fairy lights. Keep the floor clear and the palette light so the room still feels spacious, and skip anything bulky.

Should I put a real Christmas tree in the bedroom?

A small tree works beautifully in a bedroom — a tabletop tree on the nightstand or a slim tree in the corner. A full-size tree suits a large bedroom but can crowd a small one. Artificial trees are the practical choice here, since needles and watering are a nuisance in a sleeping space.

How do I hang garland on a headboard safely?

Use removable hooks or twine tied to the headboard posts, never nails or staples in the wood. Battery fairy lights are safest, since there is no trailing cord near the bed. Keep the garland light and check it weekly, and take it down before it dries out if you are using real greenery.

Can I keep Christmas bedroom decor up after Christmas?

You can keep the cosy parts — chunky knit throws, fairy lights and white faux fur — well into January, since they read as winter rather than Christmas. Pack away the plaid, the stockings and the wreaths after the New Year, and the room slides gently from festive to simply wintry.

What bedding is best for a Christmas bedroom?

Flannel and cotton plaid are the classics: warm, soft and instantly festive. Velvet throws and faux fur add luxury, while crisp white linen underneath keeps the look fresh. Choose a duvet you can wash easily, since candles, trees and extra layers mean the bedding works harder in December than usual.

How do I make a Christmas bedroom feel cosy, not cluttered?

Pick three festive elements at most — for example plaid bedding, a garland and a wreath — and keep everything else plain. Leave the nightstand mostly clear, use one colour palette, and let the fairy lights be the main sparkle. If a decoration has no clear home, it probably does not belong in the room.
